# Brag Plan: J/Dev Portfolio

## What is this app?
A portfolio that brings visual design, copy, and web development together in one place.

## The angle
Show the range without making it feel like a list of services: one clear point of view moves from a strong portfolio headline into a real project, then lands on the concise promise, "End to end."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The real headline arrives in two deliberate beats: "Designer." then "Developer." The second word locks beside the first before "End to end." completes the thought.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The portfolio's real positioning: visual design, search-driven copy, email flows, and modern engineering.
- A real portfolio project preview, Secquiz, with its cybersecurity-learning interface and published project scale.
- The `<J/Dev>` wordmark and the actual "See My Work" call to action.

## Outro / punchline
The work resolves into the portfolio's own mark and "End to end." A final "See My Work" cue makes the next step clear.

## User flow worth showing
Open the portfolio, scan the work, and choose a project to explore. This is a portfolio browsing flow, not a product demo.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a concise, confident portfolio film
- Interpretation: Use measured pacing and strong typography, with restrained motion and no inflated claims.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0a0a
- Accent: #0d9488
- Text: #ffffff
- Display font: Poppins, matching the portfolio's global typography
- Body font: Poppins, matching the portfolio's global typography
- Strongest visual element: the hero line "Designer. Developer. End to end." and the real Secquiz work preview

## Share copy (draft)
Design, copy, and code, brought together from first idea to live work.

## Audio direction
- Role: intentional quiet with a few restrained, tactile accents
- Music: none. The bundled Brag track's exact reuse terms could not be verified, so it is not included in a public-facing portfolio video.
- Music treatment: none
- Music cue guidance: not applicable
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse, low-risk CC0 accents only
- Audio-coupled moments: a soft click on the work reveal and one warm impact on the final mark
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no claims in audio, and no repeated sharp clicks

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - The promise - 3.5s
The wordmark sits small at the edge while "Designer." enters with weight. "Developer." follows and aligns into one clear statement. A subtle teal rule draws under the words.
Sequential/interaction: yes - two headline words arrive in order, then hold together for readability.
Audio intent: start quiet; one soft, low-risk accent on the second word.
Audio-coupled idea: the accent lands as "Developer." locks in.
Music: none.
Transition mood: precise cut to the work.

### Scene 2 - The range - 4.5s
The actual portfolio positioning resolves into three source-grounded disciplines: design, copy, and engineering. A thin divider draws across the frame as the three words take their places.
Sequential/interaction: yes - reveal one word at a time, then hold the full phrase.
Audio intent: restrained and tactile.
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet click as the full phrase resolves.
Music: none.
Transition mood: clean push into the project preview.

### Scene 3 - Real work - 5.5s
Show the real Secquiz project screenshot as a framed portfolio artifact, with a deliberate crop that keeps its cybersecurity-learning headline and published scale legible. The video identifies it as a portfolio project, not as a claim about this portfolio itself.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: let the image breathe; no busy sound design.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft impact as the project settles.
Music: none.
Transition mood: short, confident cut to the closing mark.

### Scene 4 - End to end - 4.5s
Return to the real `<J/Dev>` wordmark. "End to end." lands below it, followed by "See My Work." Hold the final frame cleanly.
Sequential/interaction: yes - mark first, then the promise, then the CTA; hold the complete lockup.
Audio intent: one warm, low-risk final accent and a quiet finish.
Audio-coupled idea: impact as the final mark settles.
Music: none.
Transition mood: settle on the final frame, no fade to black.
