## Frequently Asked Questions

#### "Will our non-technical team actually be able to edit the website?"
Yes. That is the entire purpose of our custom block architecture. We lock in your brand guidelines (fonts, brand colors, spacing) so non-technical team members can freely edit text, swap images, and reorder sections without accidentally ruining the design.

#### "Can you migrate our existing content and SEO rankings without losing traffic?"
Absolutely. We execute rigorous 301 redirect mapping, preserve canonical tag structures, verify XML sitemaps, and test every existing URL so you retain 100% of your accumulated Google search authority.

#### "How long does a typical build take?"
Most custom business builds take between 3 and 5 weeks from approved design to live launch, depending on the number of custom page templates required.
